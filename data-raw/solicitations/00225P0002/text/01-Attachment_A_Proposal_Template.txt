Instructions

This template is to be used for Systems Integrator Proposals. The items in [BOLDFACE] are
variables that should be changed to the specifics of the proposal in question. Notes are indicated
in italics and provide additional information or instructions. This document is to be prepared by
the contractor and submitted to the MOCS program manager, who will distribute the proposals to
the evaluation team.
The proposals will be evaluated in five key areas, which align to the five sections of the
proposal:


     1.    Relevant Project Experience
     2.    Solution Fit
     3.    Statement of Work (equivalent to Approach and Methodology)
     4.    Organization and Staffing (equivalent to Organizational Capability)
     5.    Fees (equivalent to Cost Proposal)


The weights for each area can be found in the RFS.
