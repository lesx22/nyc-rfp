## Accuracy notes (keep the narration true)

- The City Record notice for request [20241224032](https://a856-cityrecord.nyc.gov/RequestDetail/20241224032) is titled "00225P0002-City-wide E-Procurement Performance Optimization - PASSPort 2026". It is from MOCS and is dated 1/7/2025. Category 1 is optimization of the existing system; Category 2 is enhancement or replacement, up to a complete system replacement.
- The listing's due date (Feb 27, 2026) has passed, and the app shows it as **Closed**. The video presents it as the spark for the idea, not as an open bid.
- On screen, the drafting step shows the Response Studio's structured outline. Claude-written drafts arrive with Phase 3 (in progress).
- Don't claim dollar values, award winners or listing totals. None of these are verified.
